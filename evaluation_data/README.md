# Evaluation corpus

`corpora.json` freezes ten varied reference passages, ten structured comparison
passages, and two additional label-reachability probes before the criteria run.
R01 and R02 use a plain style. These are test fixtures; personal authorship is
not independently verified.

The two numeric fixture measurements are reported separately from the original
personal-writing criteria. A fixture result must not be called a measured
human false-positive rate. L02 is an identified historical human-written excerpt;
L01 is an artificial rare-word probe, not evidence of human authorship.

To evaluate the original personal-writing claims, provide ten actual personal
pieces, record their provenance, and freeze a new experiment version. Do not
overwrite these before/after artifacts or silently change their original verdicts.
